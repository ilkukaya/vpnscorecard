VPNs, proxies and Tor all hide your IP address from websites. They work very differently.

## Proxy

A proxy is a server that forwards requests for a single app, usually your browser.

- **Encryption:** often none.
- **Coverage:** only the configured app.
- **Speed:** fast.
- **Best for:** low-stakes tasks where encryption doesn't matter.

## VPN

A VPN encrypts all traffic from your device and sends it through a server run by the VPN company.

- **Encryption:** strong, for every app.
- **Coverage:** the whole device.
- **Speed:** fast with modern protocols.
- **Trust:** you rely on the provider — so choose one with an [audited no-logs policy](/guides/no-logs-vpn-explained/).
- **Best for:** everyday privacy, public Wi-Fi and travel.

## Tor

Tor sends your traffic through three volunteer relays. Each relay only knows the previous and next hop.

- **Encryption:** layered, inside the Tor network.
- **Anonymity:** the strongest of the three.
- **Speed:** slow; not suitable for streaming or large downloads.
- **Best for:** high-risk anonymity needs.

## Comparison

| | Proxy | VPN | Tor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encrypts traffic | Usually not | Yes | Yes (inside network) |
| Covers all apps | No | Yes | Tor Browser only |
| Speed | Fast | Fast | Slow |
| Anonymity | Low | Medium | High |
| Cost | Free–low | Low | Free |
