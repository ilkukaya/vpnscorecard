There are hundreds of VPNs, and most marketing sounds the same. These eight questions separate good choices from bad ones.

## 1. Can you trust its no-logs claim?

Every VPN says it keeps no logs. Look for an **independent audit** by a recognised firm, ideally repeated. Our review pages show the audit firm and year for each provider.

## 2. Where is it based?

Jurisdiction determines which laws apply. It matters less when logs genuinely don't exist. Read [5, 9 and 14 Eyes explained](/guides/five-eyes-fourteen-eyes-explained/).

## 3. Does it use modern protocols?

WireGuard, or a WireGuard-based protocol, gives the best speed on most connections. See [WireGuard vs OpenVPN](/guides/wireguard-vs-openvpn/).

## 4. Does it have the essential safety features?

- a **kill switch** ([what is a kill switch?](/guides/what-is-a-kill-switch/))
- **DNS and IPv6 leak protection**
- ideally **RAM-only servers**

## 5. Does it cover all your devices?

Check apps for every platform you use and the number of simultaneous connections. Households should look at our [best VPN for families](/best/families/) list.

## 6. What will it really cost?

- Compare the **monthly equivalent** and the **total billed** up front.
- Check the **renewal price** after the first term.
- Confirm the **refund window** and conditions.

Our [price comparison](/pricing/) shows published prices side by side.

## 7. Is it easy to use?

A VPN you forget to turn on doesn't protect you. Look for auto-connect and a clean interface — see the [best VPN for beginners](/best/beginners/).

## 8. Does it fit how you'll use it?

Different needs favour different providers: [streaming](/best/streaming/), [privacy](/best/privacy/), [gaming](/best/gaming/) or [travel](/best/travel/). Our [VPN finder quiz](/quiz/) matches you in 30 seconds.

## Things to ignore

- "Military-grade encryption" — standard AES-256.
- Huge server counts on their own — quality and locations matter more.
- Countdown timers and "today only" deals — the same discounts usually return.
