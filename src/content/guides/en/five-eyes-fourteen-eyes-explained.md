Many VPN reviews mention "Eyes" alliances. Here is what they are and how much they should affect your choice.

## The alliances

- **Five Eyes:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand. Formal intelligence-sharing dating back to the 1940s.
- **Nine Eyes:** the Five Eyes plus Denmark, France, the Netherlands and Norway.
- **Fourteen Eyes:** the Nine Eyes plus Germany, Belgium, Italy, Spain and Sweden.

These are cooperation arrangements between intelligence agencies. The details are not fully public.

## Why jurisdiction matters for a VPN

A VPN company must obey the laws of the country where it is registered. Depending on the country, authorities may be able to:

- demand user data with a court order;
- require the company to start logging a specific user;
- prevent the company from telling anyone about the request.

If data exists, it can be handed over. Intelligence sharing means data obtained in one country may reach another.

## Why logs matter more than location

A VPN that truly does not keep activity logs has nothing useful to hand over, wherever it is based. That is why our [methodology](/methodology/) rewards:

1. **Independently audited no-logs policies.**
2. **RAM-only servers**, which wipe data on reboot.
3. **Real-world evidence**, such as court cases where a provider could not produce logs.

A 14 Eyes location costs points in our privacy category, but an audited no-logs policy limits the damage.
