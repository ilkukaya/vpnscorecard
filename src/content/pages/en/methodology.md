Every VPN on VPNScorecard is scored out of **100 points** across six categories. The weights reflect what matters most for most people: privacy and performance first, then usability, network, price and streaming.

| Category | Points | What earns points |
|---|---|---|
| Speed & performance | 25 | Modern protocol technology (WireGuard or equivalent), network capacity, server quality |
| Privacy & security | 25 | Independent no-logs audits, jurisdiction, RAM-only servers, kill switch, leak protection, open-source apps |
| Ease of use | 15 | Quality of apps, setup, platform coverage, support options |
| Server network | 15 | Number of countries and servers, P2P, obfuscation and specialty servers |
| Price & value | 15 | Published prices, device limit, refund window, free plan |
| Streaming | 5 | Reported compatibility with major streaming apps |

The overall score is the sum of the six categories. We display it out of 10 (for example, 91/100 is shown as 9.1).

## Where our information comes from

We rely on evidence that anyone can check:

1. **Independent audit reports** published by the provider or the auditing firm (for example Deloitte, KPMG, Cure53 or Securitum).
2. **Provider documentation**: privacy policies, terms of service, feature pages, server lists and help-center articles.
3. **Published pricing** on each provider's own website, recorded with the date we checked it.
4. **Public reporting** from reputable technology publications and security researchers, for incidents such as data leaks or court cases.

We do not publish our own speed-test figures at this time. Speed scores reflect protocol technology and network quality, not a single lab measurement, because VPN speed varies enormously by location, time and connection.

## Privacy & security rules

- A no-logs policy only earns full credit if it has been **independently audited**.
- Headquarters in a **14 Eyes** country costs points, but an audited no-logs policy reduces the impact.
- **RAM-only servers**, a working **kill switch**, **DNS leak protection** and **open-source apps** each earn points.
- Known security incidents that were not handled transparently cost points.

## Value rules

Value is judged on the lowest published price, how many devices one subscription covers, the refund window and whether a genuinely usable free plan exists. Renewal prices and long lock-in periods are taken into account.

## How often we update

We review the scorecard at least quarterly and whenever a provider announces a major change, such as a new audit, a security incident or a new pricing structure. Every page shows when it was last updated.

## Independence

Commissions play no part in scoring. Providers cannot pay to be included, excluded or ranked higher. Read more in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
