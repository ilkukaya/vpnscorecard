## Our principles

1. **Accuracy first.** Every factual claim should be traceable to a public source such as an audit report, the provider's documentation or its price list.
2. **Transparency.** Our scoring rules are public in our [methodology](/methodology/). If a score changes, it is because the underlying facts changed.
3. **Independence.** Affiliate relationships never influence scores, rankings, pros or cons. Providers cannot review or approve our content before publication.
4. **Honesty about limits.** We clearly state what we did not measure. We do not publish invented benchmarks, fake discounts or fake user reviews.
5. **Lawful use.** We explain VPNs as privacy and security tools. We do not publish instructions for breaking the law or for evading government restrictions where VPN use is prohibited.

## How content is produced

Content is researched and written by the VPNScorecard editorial team. We may use software tools, including AI-assisted drafting and translation, to help produce and translate text; every page is checked against our data files and sources before publication, and the editorial team is responsible for everything we publish.

## Translations

VPNScorecard is published in 14 languages. Scores and facts are identical in every language; only the language changes. If a translation is unclear or wrong, please [let us know](/contact/).

## Corrections

If we make a mistake we correct it promptly and update the page's "Updated" date. Report errors through our [contact page](/contact/).

## Advertising

If we show advertising, it is clearly labelled and separate from editorial content. Advertisers have no influence over our content.
