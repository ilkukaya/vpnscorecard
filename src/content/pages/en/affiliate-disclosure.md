VPNScorecard is free to use. To pay for research, writing and hosting, we earn money in two ways:

1. **Affiliate commissions.** Many VPN providers run affiliate programs. When you click a "Visit site" or similar button and later buy a subscription, the provider may pay us a commission. You pay the same price — sometimes less, if the provider offers a partner discount.
2. **Advertising.** Some pages may display clearly labelled ads served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense.

## What this means for our rankings

- Commissions have **no influence** on scores or rankings. Scores are calculated from the rules in our [methodology](/methodology/).
- We include and rank VPNs that pay us **nothing**. For example, Mullvad has no affiliate program and ranks in our top ten.
- A provider cannot pay to be included, to be ranked higher or to have negative points removed.

## How to recognise affiliate links

Outbound links to VPN providers go through addresses that start with `/go/`. These links are marked as sponsored for search engines. Where a provider has no affiliate program, the link simply takes you to its official website.

## Regional restrictions

Where VPN use is banned or restricted by law, outbound links to VPN providers are disabled for visitors from those countries. See [VPN legality by country](/vpn-legality/).
