VPNScorecard exists for one reason: **most "best VPN" lists are impossible to check.** Rankings change with commission rates, scores appear without explanation, and "exclusive coupons" expire the moment you click.

We take a different approach. Every VPN on this site gets a score out of 100, built from six weighted categories, and every point comes from information you can verify yourself: published independent audits, the company's legal jurisdiction, documented security features, protocol technology, network size and published prices.

## What we do

- **Score VPNs transparently.** Our [methodology](/methodology/) explains exactly how points are awarded.
- **Show our sources.** Facts come from providers' own documentation, public audit reports and published price lists, and we show the date they were last checked.
- **Keep rankings independent.** Providers cannot pay for a position or a better score. Mullvad, which has no affiliate program at all, sits in our top ten.
- **Write for normal people.** Our [guides](/guides/) explain privacy and security in plain language.

## What we don't do

- We don't invent coupon codes or fake countdown timers.
- We don't publish benchmark numbers we did not measure.
- We don't encourage anyone to use a VPN to break the law. Check our [VPN legality by country](/vpn-legality/) page before you travel.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you buy a VPN through them, the provider may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. That income keeps the site free. It never decides the order of our rankings — see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) and [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Found a mistake?

VPN companies change prices, features and policies often. If you spot something out of date, please [tell us](/contact/) and we will correct it.
