We are a privacy website, so we try to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what we do collect and why.

## Who we are

VPNScorecard ("we", "us") operates this website. You can reach us through our [contact page](/contact/).

## Data we collect

**When you browse the site**

- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept for a short period.
- **Analytics.** If enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that do not use cookies and do not track you across websites. If we use Google Analytics, it only runs according to your consent choices.

**When you use our tools**

- **What is my IP.** To show your IP address and approximate location, we read them from your request at the moment you open the page. We do not store or log them.
- **VPN finder quiz.** Your answers are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

**When you contact us**

- If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message through Netlify Forms. We use them only to reply and delete them when they are no longer needed.

**When you click a link to a VPN provider**

- Links to providers pass through our `/go/` redirect. If you then buy a subscription, the provider and its affiliate network may use cookies or tracking parameters to credit the sale. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Cookies and advertising

We do not set advertising cookies ourselves. If we display ads from Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a certified consent-management platform before any such cookies are used. You can manage Google's use of advertising cookies at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar laws (such as Turkey's KVKK or Brazil's LGPD) apply, we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and securing the website, your consent (for advertising and non-essential analytics cookies) and, for contact messages, the steps needed to answer your request.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data-protection authority. California residents may ask what personal information we have and request its deletion; we do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, [contact us](/contact/).

## International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
